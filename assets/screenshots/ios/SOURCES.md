# iOS catalog and preview sources

The gallery contains the 44 distinct games in the catalog bundled with
[LSX4 iOS JIT 128.9775](https://github.com/lpfoj/lsx4/releases/tag/ios-jit-128.9775),
checked on November 3, 2026. Its `ios-gameplay-observations.json` contains 45 title
IDs; the two Sonic Mania regions share one gallery card and compatibility row.
[catalog.json](catalog.json) records the titles, IDs and preview sources.

The bundled list combines earlier iPhone gameplay observations with community
reports. Balatro and Farlands Journey are labelled as community-reported rather
than independently verified results. Bloodborne also has a
[newer iOS gameplay recording](https://youtu.be/uIZNsICj8PQ).

Previews come from screenshots attached to the public iOS Playable feed at
<https://lsx45.app/api/community-reports?limit=24&os=iOS&result=playable>.
Islets has an attached screenshot. The other 43 cards use the app's LSX4 logo,
not an Android screenshot or a game cover. The logo comes from the same published
iOS application. It is a placeholder, not gameplay evidence.

The compatibility table also keeps the separately published iOS demonstrations
for [The Last of Us Remastered](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20qtM8hfL0I) and
[God of War: Sons of Sparta, Dreaming Sarah and Void Terrarium++](https://youtu.be/la1JBTy7qj8).
These four games are additional video results, not extra entries in the 44-game
bundled catalog. A result for one host platform does not establish the result on
the other, and catalog entries do not guarantee performance throughout a game.
